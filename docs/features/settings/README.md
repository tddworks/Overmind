# Settings

Open it from the popover: **⚙ → Settings…**. Changes save at once.

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Account** | the connected account and its plan · *Disconnect* · the Worker in focus |
| **Reading** | *Read every 5 · 10 · 15 · 30 minutes*. *STALE* shows after two missed reads |
| **Warnings** | *Warn me at 50% 75% 90%* · *Warn on pace* · *Notifications* |
| **General** | *Start at login* · *Check for updates automatically* · *Check now* |

Everything but *Start at login* and updates lives in `~/.edgemeter/settings.json`.
